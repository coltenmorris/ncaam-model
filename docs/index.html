How to read this

Units

A unit is one standard bet. Units = profit or loss if you bet 1 unit on every pick in a group. Against the spread at the usual -110 price, a win pays +0.91, a loss costs -1, and a push is 0. So +5.00 units means you would be up five bets' worth; with $100 bets that is +$500. Because a loss costs more than a win pays, you need to win 52.4% just to break even. Market picks are paid at the market's own price instead of -110.
